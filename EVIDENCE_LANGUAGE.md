# Scientific Evidence Language Rules

## 1. Allowed Conservative Language
- *"Observed increase in conductivity with increasing temperature in the tested range."*
- *"Within the analyzed Project 7 dataset (N=8), conductivity showed a statistically detectable positive association with substrate temperature."*
- *"Estimated positive temperature effect under the specified experimental design."*

## 2. Prohibited Unsupported Language
- *"Temperature causes conductivity to increase."*
- *"Temperature is proven to increase conductivity."*
- *"This proves optimal thin film performance."*
